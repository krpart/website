# Your website: how to make changes

Your website is **krinapatel.art**. It has six pages: Home, About, Studio, Research, Books and Contact. You change all of them in one place, **Pages CMS**, a free editing page that shows every part of your website as a labelled box.

---

## 1. The short version

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org** and click **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Click your website, then click **Website** on the left.
3. Open the page you want (for example **About page**), click the box you want to change and type.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Wait one minute, then open krinapatel.art and refresh the page.

---

## 2. Signing in

You sign in with your GitHub account. GitHub is the service that stores your website.

1. Open **app.pagescms.org** in your browser.
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub**.
   You now see the GitHub sign-in page (if you are already signed in to GitHub, skip to step 5).
3. Type your GitHub username or email, and your password. Click **Sign in**.
4. GitHub may ask for a code or a passkey. Enter the code from your phone or email, or approve the passkey on your phone.
   You are now back in Pages CMS.
5. If Pages CMS asks for permission to use your GitHub account, click **Authorize**.
6. Click your website in the list. (A "repository" is GitHub's word for the folder that holds your website. Yours is **USERNAME/REPO**.)
   You now see **Website** in the menu on the left.

---

## 3. Changing words

Example: changing your bio.

1. Sign in (section 2) and click **Website** on the left.
   You see one group for each page, in the same order as the menu: **Name, email and links (every page)**, **Home page**, **About page**, **Studio page**, **Research page**, **Books page**, **Contact page**.
2. Open **About page**, then **Short bio**. Each paragraph has its own box.
3. Click in the paragraph you want to change and type your new words.
4. Click **Save** at the top of the page.
   A short message says the change is saved.
5. Check your change (section 7).

The same steps work for every piece of text:

- Your statement: **Home page** → **Statement (the italic paragraph)** → **Statement**
- A book description: **Books page** → **Books** → open the book → **One or two lines about the book**
- An artwork caption: **Studio page** → **Artworks** → open the artwork → **Title**, **Year**, **Medium** or **Size**
- Your projects list: **About page** → **Projects** → open a project → **Year**, **Project name** or **Place or short description**

Tips:

- Type plain text only. Italics are set for you: book titles go in **Words in italics** on the same page, written exactly as they appear in the text.
- To turn some words in your bio into a link, add them to **Linked words** (on the **About page**), with the web address.

## 4. Adding, removing or reordering a link

Example: adding a "Buy in India" button to your book.

1. Sign in and click **Website**.
2. Open **Books page**, then **Books**, then click the book you want to change.
3. Under **India buy links**, click the add button (it has a **+** sign) (check).
   A new, empty link appears.
4. In **Button text**, type the words for the button, for example `Buy in India`.
5. In **Web address**, paste the full address, starting with `https://`.
6. Click **Save**.
7. Tick **Show India book links** near the top of **Books page**, then click **Save** again.

To remove a link: click the remove button next to it (a bin or **×**) (check), then click **Save**.

To change the order: drag the link up or down by its handle (the dotted grip on the left) (check), then click **Save**.

The same works for **Buy links**, **Artworks**, **Projects** and **Events**.

## 6. Changing or adding an image

### First, make the photo smaller

Large photos make the website slow, especially on phones in India. Shrink every new photo first. This takes two minutes in your browser and is free.

1. Go to **squoosh.app**.
2. Drag your photo onto the page, or click the button to choose it from your computer (check).
   You now see your photo, with settings on the right.
3. On the right, switch on **Resize**.
4. Type `1600` in whichever box is the bigger number, **Width** or **Height**. The other number changes by itself.
5. Under **Compress**, choose **WebP**.
6. Set **Quality** to `80`.
7. Click the download button (an arrow pointing down) at the bottom right.
8. Rename the file with plain words and the year, for example `river-study-2026.webp`.

### Then, put it on your website

Example: adding a new artwork to the Studio page.

1. Sign in and click **Website**.
2. Open **Studio page**, then **Artworks**. Click the add button (**+**) (check).
3. Next to **Image**, click to open the image picker.
   You now see the images already on your website.
4. Click **Upload** and choose the file you made with Squoosh (check).
5. Select your new image.
6. Fill in **Title**, **Year**, **Medium** and **Size**.
7. Fill in **Description for blind visitors**: one or two sentences saying what the artwork looks like.
8. Leave **Phone-size copy**, **Image width in pixels** and **Image height in pixels** empty. Danny can fill these in later.
9. Click **Save**.

To add your portrait when the Open Studios photo is ready, use **About page** → **Portrait** → **Portrait photo**, then tick **Show your portrait**.

## 7. Checking your change

1. Wait one minute after clicking **Save**.
2. Open **krinapatel.art** and go to the page you changed.
3. Refresh the page: on a phone, pull the page down; on a computer, press **Ctrl + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + R** (Mac).
4. If you don't see the change, wait two more minutes and refresh again.

## 8. Undoing a mistake

The easiest way: text Danny. Every save is kept, so nothing is ever lost.

To do it yourself, there are two parts.

### Part A: find the version before your mistake

1. Go to **github.com/USERNAME/REPO** and sign in if asked.
2. Click **content.json** in the list of files. (This file holds all the words on your website.)
3. Click **History** at the top right of the file.
   You see a list of saves, newest at the top. The top one is your last save.
4. Click the save just below it (the one before your mistake).
5. Click **Browse files** (or the **<>** button) to see the website as it was then (check).
6. Click **content.json**.
7. Click the **Copy raw file** button (two overlapping squares) above the file.

### Part B: put it back

1. Go to **github.com/USERNAME/REPO/blob/main/content.json**.
2. Click the pencil button, **Edit this file**.
3. Click inside the text, select everything (**Ctrl + A** or **Cmd + A**) and paste (**Ctrl + V** or **Cmd + V**).
4. Click **Commit changes…** at the top right.
5. Click **Commit changes** in the box that opens.
6. Check your website (section 7).

## 9. Backup method: editing on GitHub directly

Use this only if Pages CMS is not working.

1. Go to **github.com/USERNAME/REPO/blob/main/content.json**.
2. Click the pencil button, **Edit this file**.
3. Find the words you want to change. Change only the words between the quote marks.
4. Click **Commit changes…**, then **Commit changes** again in the box that opens.
5. Check your website (section 7).

Three rules, so the file keeps working:

- **Keep the quote marks.** Every piece of text sits between `"` and `"`.
- **Keep the commas.** Don't add or remove any commas between lines.
- **Change only the words.** Leave every name before a colon (like `"heading":`) as it is.

If a page shows only your name and one line after a change, a quote mark or comma went missing. Undo the change (section 8) or text Danny.

## 10. Changing the link preview

The link preview is the picture and words that appear when someone shares your website in WhatsApp, iMessage or LinkedIn. Danny normally changes this. These steps are a backup.

Each page has its own file: **index.html** is the home page, then **about.html**, **studio.html**, **research.html**, **books.html** and **contact.html**.

**To change the words:**

1. Go to **github.com/USERNAME/REPO** and click the file for the page, for example **index.html**.
2. Click the pencil button, **Edit this file**.
3. Find the line that starts `<meta property="og:title"` and change the words between the quote marks after `content=`.
4. Do the same for `og:description` two lines below.
5. Click **Commit changes…**, then **Commit changes**.

**To change the picture** (used by every page):

1. Make a picture exactly 1200 pixels wide and 630 pixels tall, saved as a JPG.
2. Name it exactly `share-image.jpg`.
3. On your GitHub page, click **Add file**, then **Upload files**.
4. Drag in your `share-image.jpg` and click **Commit changes**.

WhatsApp remembers old previews for a while, so the new one can take a day or more to show.
